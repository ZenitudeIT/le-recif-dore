welcome

Arrive for the lagoon.
Stay for the sunsets.

Le Récif Doré sits on the sheltered north-west shore of Mauritius, in the fishing village of Pointe aux Piments. Trade winds pass the bay by, the water stays calm and clear, and each evening the sun sinks straight into the sea in front of you.

Récif doré means “golden reef”. It's the name for the moment at dusk when the coral under the lagoon catches the last of the light, and it's the feeling we want every guest to take home.
